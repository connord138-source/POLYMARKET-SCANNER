Polymarket by building on our model, not just on betting, but any kind of
profit whatsoever." This is the evidence-based answer. Everything below was
checked against primary sources where one exists; where only third-party
pages exist that is said.

auto-trader has 250 settled trades, 48% win rate, -$27.72 lifetime P&L

sellable assets are the tooling: the DraftKings value board with its graded
record, the whale-wallet ledger, the Agent Crew, and the scanner UI.

## Details and sources

### 1. Polymarket US affiliate program
- Official: docs.polymarket.us/incentives/referral.md — "This program rewards
  approved affiliates for referring new Participants to Polymarket US. To
  learn more, contact affiliate@polymarket.com." No public terms.
- Evidence deals exist: Covers (code COVERS), SI (SIBONUS), iGaming Future
  (IFUTURE) all run Polymarket US promo codes through tracked `/visit/` links.
- Consumer refer-a-friend (separate): $25 trading credit each for referrer and
  friend after ID verification and a $10 deposit; up to 50 referrals, $1,250
  max; credits are not withdrawable until traded (SI, Oct 2026).
- Polymarket US fee schedule (docs.polymarket.us/fees.md, effective Oct 7
  2026): taker fee = 0.0695 × C × p × (1−p), maker rebate 0.0125 × C × p × (1−p);
  monthly taker rebates 10/25/50% above $250k/$1M/$10M prior-month taker volume.
- Action: email affiliate@polymarket.com describing edgerunner.vip (traffic,
  audience, the Polymarket market links the scanner already shows). Add
  `?src=` tagging to outbound Polymarket links so clicks are measurable.

### 2. Kalshi publisher deal
- No public affiliate program. Kalshi's referral FAQ
  (help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823783): rewards "may be issued as trading
  credits with withdrawal restrictions", "available only to eligible U.S.
  users", limits "vary by program". A third-party affiliate review states
  Kalshi publishes no commission and negotiates privately; the "20%
  commission" offers online belong to unaffiliated tools, not Kalshi.
- Publishers run codes anyway (Covers COVERS60, SI VIP60, TheLines
  PREMARLINES), so negotiated deals exist.
- Action: ask via kalshi.com/builders ("Learn more and apply") or the Kalshi
  Discord `#dev` channel; pitch the DraftKings value board + Kalshi links.

### 3. Kalshi Builder Codes (the strongest build option)
- Primary: news.kalshi.com/p/kalshi-solana-tokenized-predictions — tokenized
  Kalshi markets live on Solana "via Jupiter and DFlow"; "Builders can now earn
  fees and rewards that scale with volume"; "More than $2 million in builder
  grants are now open"; "Learn more and apply" at kalshi.com/builders (that
  page rate-limited every fetch from this session: HTTP 429).
- Kalshi-sponsored grant listing (superteam.fun/earn/grants/kalshi-grants):
  "Builders integrating third-party toolkits (such as DFlow and Jupiter) can
  earn up to 30% of fees generated by their users, and can charge additional
  fees on top. Interactions settle on Solana." Grants "up to 10k USDC";
  listing status: closed; "Access to trading on Kalshi itself may be subject
  to regional restrictions."
- How users trade: DFlow Prediction Markets API (metadata
  `dev-prediction-markets-api.dflow.net`, trade `dev-quote-api.dflow.net`,
  production needs an `x-api-key`). "Federal regulations require that all
  participants in Kalshi's prediction markets be identity-verified" — wallets
  must pass DFlow Proof KYC; "Unverified wallets are blocked from buying
  outcome tokens" (QuickNode guide). DFlow spot docs expose `platformFeeBps`
  + `feeAccount` for integrator fees; whether the same parameters apply to
  prediction-market orders is not stated in the public docs — ask DFlow.
- Why not the native Kalshi API: Kalshi Developer Agreement v1.1 limits API
  use to "facilitating a member's own trading on the Exchange; all other usages
  are disallowed", and prohibits facilitating trading by other members,
  sublicensing, and sharing API data without written authorization. Routing
  other people's orders needs the builder/partner path, not our API key.
- Sizing: Kalshi taker fee ≈ 0.07 × C × p × (1−p) per third-party guides
  (Kalshi's own fee PDF also rate-limited). At p = 0.50 that is ≈ 1.75% of
  notional; 30% of it ≈ 0.5% of routed notional, i.e. ≈ $500 per $100k routed,
  before any platform fee we add.
- Honest caveat: this is on-chain (Solana wallets, USDC). Our audience today is
  DraftKings/CFB bettors; conversion to a Solana wallet + KYC flow is unproven.

### 6. Polymarket (international) Builder program
- Official fees page (docs.polymarket.com/programs/builders/fees.md): taker

- Tiers (docs.polymarket.com/developers/builders/builder-tiers): Unverified

- Scale (builders.polymarket.com leaderboard, 1-month volume): #1 Betmoar

  0.5–1% of attributed volume; Polymarket has not published the formula).
- Blocker: Polymarket's global exchange does not serve US persons. A builder

- Also: Polymarket referral program (docs.polymarket.com/programs/referral-program)

### 7. Polymarket US ISV partner
- docs.polymarket.us/partners: ISV "provides the software experience for
  retail traders. Not a broker." "ISVs are not brokers and carry no broker
  registration." Partner is "a pure message facilitator"; "You never hold
  participant funds." Orders are submitted "as the Firm, on behalf of the
  participant" with an `x-participant-id` header.
- Monetization: **vendor fees** — "what you charge a Retail Participant for an
  order, per your agreement with them", a fixed USD amount declared on each
  order, collected at most daily by a `VENDOR_FEES` transfer. No cap stated.
- The bar: signed ISV Connectivity Agreement; participant clickthrough
  agreements; Partner Funding (Beta) requires "a separate legal entity in your
  corporate structure — not by your firm" that "custodies participant cash
  off-platform" (typically "the wallet provider affiliated with your firm"),
  a three-party agreement, and that entity "must exist and be active before
  your firm's API credentials can be enabled". Contact
  institutional@polymarket.us.
- Verdict: the only fully-US-legal way to earn per-trade fees from our
  signals, but it is a fintech-company path (entity, funding partner, legal).
  Worth one email to learn whether a small firm is accepted; not a next sprint.

# Part 2 — the model itself earning (Oct 12 2026, live scans)

The operator's clarification: "profit" meant the software model making money
on Kalshi and Polymarket by any mechanism, not selling the software. So the
structural, non-forecasting ways a bot can earn were each measured against
live books on Oct 12 2026 (scripts kept in the session scratchpad; numbers
below are what they printed).

| Mechanism | What was measured | Result |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| Neg-risk "Dutch book" (buy NO on every outcome when Σ YES bids > $1; buy every YES when Σ asks < $1) | 800 highest-volume events, 377 neg-risk events with ≥3 live markets, 141 fully quoted | Best Σbid 1.011 (NHL 2027 champion, 2 sets). 9 events above 1.000, one above 1.01, none above 1.03. Every leg pays a taker fee (politics 10% × p(1−p)), so the gross 0.1–1.1c per set is below cost. **Dead.** |
| Strike monotonicity on sports totals (Over L_low must be ≥ Over L_high) | 948 sports events, 2,459 total markets, 15,168 strike pairs | 0 violations, 0 within half a cent. **Dead.** |
| Kalshi vs Polymarket moneylines | 103 games matched (92 NCAAF, 7 MLB, 4 WNBA) | 0 real crossings. Median mid difference ≈ 1c; 5 of 203 sides differed by > 2c. **Dead pregame.** |
| Kalshi vs Polymarket totals, same strike and cross-strike | 27 NCAAF games, 10,773 strike pairs, 227 same-line pairs | Median mid difference 0.5c; 4 pairs with a 1–2c raw gap, none positive after Kalshi's 7% × p(1−p) taker fee plus Polymarket's 3% × p(1−p). **Dead pregame.** |
| Settlement-lag harvesting (buy the decided side of a finished game before resolution) | 1,463 open sports markets kicked off 3.5–72h earlier, books read directly | 2 sides bid ≥ 0.90, both with no ask at all. Finished games are resolved or asked at > 0.995 before a 5-minute bot sees them. **Dead.** |
| "Sure things" within 96h | 268 markets with an askable side at ≤ 0.995 and $28M of depth | The 4–5% "yields" are 95% probabilities, not mispricings (exact-score NOs, weather bands, BTC levels). Not an edge. |
| Speed (how much being as fast as the whale is worth) | `/speed/report`, 571 signals | Average detection lag 324s costs 0.21c; ROI at the whale's own price −0.3%, at ours −0.1%. The whales we copy do not have an edge to be fast on. |

Where the ledger actually stands: 250 settled paper trades, 48% wins,
−$27.72, **and the paper ledger pays neither spread nor fees** (fills at
Gamma's price, exits at the CLOB midpoint). Raw whale signals in D1
(`signals_log`): 11,343 WIN / 8,781 LOSS = 56.4% at an average entry of
56.6c, i.e. breakeven. Real money on these trades would do worse than paper
by roughly the spread (NFL books average 1.8c) plus the taker fee.

## What is left, and what was built for it

1. **Be the maker (execution).** Polymarket's sports fee schedule is
   `rate 0.03 × p(1−p)`, taker-only, with a 15% maker rebate; Polymarket US
   pays makers a `0.0125 × p(1−p)` rebate; Kalshi charges 0 maker fee on most
   series. On the last 30 paper trades ($151 staked, −$9.29), a 2c better entry
   is worth +$6.58 — the same order as the loss. Built: `src/execution.js`, a
   shadow ledger on every paper entry: it stores the live bid/ask and fee
   schedule, prices a **taker** fill (ask + fee) and a **maker** fill (resting
   bid, counted filled only if the tape printed a sell at or below it within
   `execShadowTtlMin`, default 60 min, plus the rebate), and settles both
   against the real exit. `GET /autotrader/execution` (in the backend
   snapshot as `execution`). Decision rule: after ~50 settled shadows, if
   `makerMinusTakerOnFilled` is clearly positive and `fillRate` is above
   ~50%, switch the live-path order type to resting limit orders; if
   `taker` is far below `paper`, the paper ledger is overstating the bot and
   the go-live milestone must be re-read on the taker ledger.
2. **Kalshi in-game lag (the only legal real-money venue question).** Kalshi
   is US-legal to trade through its own API (Developer Agreement v1.1 allows
   a member's own trading). Pregame, Kalshi tracks Polymarket to within 1c.
   Whether it lags in-game, when prices move 5–20c a minute, is unmeasured.
   Built: `src/xvenue.js`, a one-minute cron (`"* * * * *"`, sampler only)
   that matches Kalshi game markets to Polymarket moneylines
   (NFL/CFB/NBA/NHL/MLB/WNBA, city↔nickname aliases) and records both
   venues' top of book for every live game side into D1 `xvenue_quotes`
   (pruned after 14 days). `GET /xvenue/report?hours=48&moveC=3` reports,
   per sport: how often a ≥3c Polymarket move left Kalshi's same-minute
   quote crossable after Kalshi's taker fee (`staleRate`,
   `avgStaleEdgeC`), who moves first, and both venues' spreads. Decision
   rule: a Kalshi lane (take stale quotes, or quote around Polymarket's mid
   with 0 maker fee) is worth building only if `staleRate × avgStaleEdgeC`
   is meaningfully positive across a week of games.
3. **Not worth building** (measured above): neg-risk arbitrage, strike
   monotonicity, cross-venue pregame arbitrage, settlement-lag sniping,
   liquidity-reward market making (Track A), faster whale copying.

Scripts: `scratchpad/edge/{negrisk,lines,xvenue,xlines,lag,surething}.mjs`
in the Oct 12 session; rerun them when markets are live (Saturday CFB,
Sunday NFL) before trusting a pregame-only conclusion.
